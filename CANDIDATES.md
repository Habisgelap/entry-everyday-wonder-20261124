# Candidates — Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line

Scored 1–5 on each judge criterion (in the judge's order): 1. small moment of
wonder in one sentence; 2. angle the reader has not met; 3. surprise lands in
one read; 4. grounded in something real and specific; 5. no filler, no worn
phrase. Max 25. Character counts verified with node.

Winner: **C7 (24/25)**. The mirror confusion is the sharpest single-read
surprise: an everyday lift mirror, a stranger beside you, and one second of
not knowing which reflection is yours. C1 and C10 lose on freshness because
they reuse the brief's own example images (lift door, kettle click).
