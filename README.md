One sentence, at most 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in
everyday life.

> "The lift mirror showed me standing next to a stranger, and for a second I could not tell which of us was me."

`main.ts` writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. Built for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/competitions/u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3)
as [@ipins](https://make.fun/r/mx1zgjg0).
